- **What is still missing:** today that consent is given together with accepting this Policy, not in a separate checkbox only for this data, and that is also how those who accepted an earlier version gave it. A specific checkbox for this consent will come in a future version of the app, not yet scheduled.
- **How to withdraw it:** by deleting that data or your account (Section 10). Withdrawing it does not invalidate what was done before.

## 7. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

- **Encryption in transit:** All communications use HTTPS/TLS
- **Encryption at rest on our servers:** Google encrypts stored data with AES-256
- **Secure authentication:** Firebase Auth with JWT tokens
- **Security rules:** Strict per-user data access
- **Encrypted data on your phone:** your favorite activities and the record of completed activities are stored in a local database encrypted with AES-256, whose key is kept in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain); your date of birth and preferences as a guest, in that same secure storage
- **Data without additional encryption on your phone:** your recent searches and, depending on the app version, some guest data (for example, favorites or consent choices) are stored without their own encryption, protected only by the isolation the operating system gives each app. In version 1.0.0 (build 10) of the app, deleting your account does not remove them from the phone: they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data
- **Guide audio stored on your phone:** the app keeps the guide audio it downloads so it does not have to download it again. It is the same audio for everyone and contains nothing of yours, but which files are stored shows which activities you listened to: in version 1.0.0 (build 10) each file name indicates the activity; in later versions the names no longer show it at a glance. The app tries to keep this space under about 100 MB (in version 1.0.0 that limit only counts what was downloaded in each use, so it can take up more). **Deleting your account does not remove this audio, in any version of the app.** To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data from your phone settings (on Android, «Clear storage» or «Clear data»; «Clear cache» is not enough)

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights over your personal data:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your data

- **Object:** Object to processing in certain cases

- **Withdraw consent:** At any time

### 🇺🇸 California (CCPA/CPRA)

- **Know:** What data we collect and how we use it
- **Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate personal data
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal data
- **Opt-out:** Opt out of data sale (note: we don't sell data)
- **Limit:** Limit the use and disclosure of your sensitive personal information — your data about intimate activity preferences and sessions qualifies as sensitive personal information under CPRA; we only use it to provide you the service
- **Non-discrimination:** Same service regardless of your privacy choices

### 🇧🇷 Brazil (LGPD)

- **Confirmation:** Know if we process your data
- **Access:** Obtain a copy of your data
- **Correction:** Update incomplete or inaccurate data
- **Anonymization:** Request anonymization of unnecessary data
- **Deletion:** Delete data processed with consent
- **Portability:** Transfer data to another provider
- **Revocation:** Withdraw consent at any time

### 🇦🇷🇺🇾 Argentina and Uruguay

Under Law 25.326 (Argentina) and Law 18.331 (Uruguay), you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your personal data.

## 10. How to Delete Your Account and Data

The fastest way is from the app: deletion is immediate. If you cannot use the app, request it by email (Option 2); you do not need to reinstall it.

### Option 1: From the app (registered users)

1. Open Íntima and go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Delete Account**
3. Confirm the deletion

Before deleting, the app asks you to confirm it is you: your password or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, choosing that account again. If you forgot your password, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen, or request deletion by email (Option 2).

### Option 2: By email, without the app

Use it if you no longer have the app installed, cannot get into your account, or prefer to ask in writing. Write to intimaapp@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and include:

- Your account email and how you signed in (email and password, Google or Apple)
- That you want your account and all its data deleted

So that no one can delete someone else's account, we verify that the account is yours: if you write from the account's email, that is enough; otherwise we write to that email and wait for your confirmation. If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, your account is registered with an address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com; you can find it in your Apple ID settings, under "Sign in with Apple".

When we delete by email, we delete your account and all the data in the list below that is on our servers. Error reports we cannot delete before they expire, after 90 days (see Section 8). What is stored on your phone we cannot delete from here: it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you are requesting deletion of the account of a person who has died, write to us with documentation showing your relationship and we will handle it case by case.

Because a guest account is not linked to any email, we cannot identify it by email: see the next section.

### Guest users (no registration)

If you use the app without creating an account:
- Depending on the app version, your guest data is stored only on your phone or also on our servers, linked to an anonymous identifier
- It is kept for as long as your guest account exists; there is no automatic deletion for inactivity
- Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or tapping **Exit guest mode** removes or makes inaccessible the copy on your phone, but **does not delete** what may have been stored on our servers
- To delete everything: tap **Create Account** in Settings. The app asks what to do with your guest data: if you choose to discard it, the app tries to delete it right then; if you choose to keep it, it moves to your account and you can delete it along with the account from **Settings → Delete Account**. Do this before uninstalling the app
- **In version 1.0.0 (build 10) of the app**, that is the only way to discard your guest data from the app: it requires completing the creation of a new account (in that version the discard button appears in Spanish, «Descartar invitado»). Also, in that version discarding does not delete the guest favorites or searches stored on your phone (they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data); in later versions it does. Afterwards you can delete that account from **Settings → Delete Account**. Also in that version, if while in guest mode you sign in to an account you already had, the app does not ask you anything: whatever you had saved as a guest on our servers stays there under the anonymous identifier, without being deleted (see the last point). In later versions, the app asks you first what to do with that data
- In any version, if deletion on our servers fails, the app signs out of the guest session without telling you and continues creating the account or signing in to your account. In that case, whatever was on our servers remains under the anonymous identifier, as explained in the next point
- If you already uninstalled it, that data remains under an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your name or your email: neither you nor we can connect it to you

> ⏱️ **Timeframes:** Deletion from the app is **immediate and irreversible**: there is no grace period, it cannot be cancelled, and the data cannot be recovered. If something fails midway, the app tells you and you can try again. If you request it by email, we do it within 5 business days after we receive your request and confirm when it is done. If within that period we could not verify that the account is yours, we tell you within the same period, with the reason and what we need, and delete as soon as you confirm.

### What gets deleted?

- ✅ Your profile and preferences
- ✅ Session history
- ✅ Submitted feedback
- ✅ Your plans
- ✅ Favorite activities
- ✅ Authentication data
- ✅ Your favorites and activity record stored on your phone (only when deleting from the app; everything else the app stores on your phone, such as your recent searches, is deleted depending on the version: see Section 7)

### What CANNOT be deleted?

- Aggregated and anonymized data already incorporated into statistics
- Usage events already sent to Google, if you agreed to them: we do not delete them one by one. They may carry session or plan identifiers; once you delete your account we do not use them to link them to you, and they expire on their own between 2 and 3 months later (Google deletes expired data in a monthly cycle)
- Error reports, if you agreed to them (see the exception in Section 3.2): they expire on their own after 90 days and today we cannot delete them before then, not even if you request deletion by email. They may include the internal identifier of your account; once the account is deleted, we do not use it to link those reports to you
- Guide audio stored on your phone: deleting your account does not remove it, in any version of the app (see Section 7)
- Information required by legal obligations
